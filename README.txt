JUGNU MUNSHI v0.5: how to put it online (5 minutes, free)

1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop on a computer.
2. Drag this whole "jugnu-app" folder onto the page.
3. Netlify gives you a link like https://something.netlify.app. Rename it in Site settings if you like.
4. Open the link on the shopkeeper's phone in Chrome once, with internet.
5. Chrome menu > "Add to Home screen". Jugnu now opens like an app.

After step 4, Jugnu works with NO internet: entries, balances, small talk,
shop health, tips, receipts and statement PDFs, and WhatsApp reminder text.
Internet is only needed for the AI advisor and for actually sending the WhatsApp message.

All data stays on that phone. Take a backup from Settings once a week.

FAMILY (v0.5)
- Tap your name at the top of the Jugnu screen to add up to 3 children.
- Set a 4-digit Khata PIN: children never see the khata or money.
- Children get Ustad Jugnu: offline quizzes (Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English),
  offline formulas, and an AI tutor when online (20 questions a day on the free plan).
- Your weekly report for each child is on the Report tab, with a WhatsApp button.
